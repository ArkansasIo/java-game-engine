# UniversCivilization: Empire at War - Campaign and Systems Blueprint

This document defines the core mechanics, worldbuilding, and systems design for *UniversCivilization: Empire at War*, powered by the Arklight Game Engine. It merges RTS, 4X, and MMORPG elements into a cohesive Sci-Fi Space Universe Conquest experience.


## Scale

- 12 acts
- 50 chapters per act, 600 chapter slots total
- 35 required missing/detail elements per chapter
- 90 classes/subclasses across weapons, shields, armor, units, military, civil, government, and buildings

## 35 Required Chapter Elements

1. Setting
2. Weather
3. Time Period
4. Political Context
5. Economic State
6. Cultural Tension
7. Religious Influence
8. Technological Level
9. Special Power System
10. Military Presence
11. Civilian Life
12. Government Stance
13. Key Character

## Interface Pillars

- Galaxy view: empire borders, hyperlanes, fleets, anomalies, claims, trade routes, and war fronts.
- System view: planets, stations, orbital lanes, tactical fleets, civilian traffic, and combat zones.
- Planet view: districts, buildings, population, stability, production, defense, culture, and missions.
- Commander view: RTS unit control, control groups, abilities, stance, formations, build queues, and tactical overlays.
- Character view: MMORPG avatar, equipment, class tree, social guild/faction systems, quests, and reputation.
- Government view: laws, policies, budgets, diplomacy, espionage, leader assignments, and crisis directives.
- Economy view: resource flows, shortages, trade, industry, logistics, upkeep, and market orders.
- War room: fronts, objectives, armies, fleets, intel, orders, rally points, invasions, and occupation status.
